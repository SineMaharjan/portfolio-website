01 / THE MINDSET

Curiosity is
the starting point.

I’m more interested in understanding the system than memorizing the answer. The process matters: ask, investigate, test, learn, improve.

“Don't stop at what works. Find out why it works — and how it could work better.”

I’m Sine, a Networking & Cybersecurity student at Islington College, studying on a London Metropolitan University–affiliated programme. My interests span cybersecurity, software development, and the systems that connect them.

I’m ambitious about where I want to go, curious about what I don’t know yet, and resilient enough to treat setbacks as feedback. I want to build practical things, develop real technical depth, and keep raising my own standard.
